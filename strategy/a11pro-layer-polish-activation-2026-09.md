# A11pro Layer Polish & Activation — 2026-09

## Objective

Turn the research and profit strategy into a controlled operating system that is useful immediately, measurable at every layer, visually coherent, secure by default, and never allowed to imply activity that has not been verified.

Operating rule:

VERIFY → PROVE → PROTECT → PROPOSE → OWNER APPROVE → EXECUTE → VERIFY → RECORD

No hidden background process, automatic spend, irreversible commercial change, or external commitment is implied by this design.

## Layer 0 — Authority and control

Polish:
- One owner identity, one approval vocabulary, one decision queue.
- Every material action has status: PROPOSED, APPROVED, EXECUTING, VERIFIED, BLOCKED, FAILED, UNVERIFIED.
- Separate recommendation from execution.

Activate:
- Central approval record with actor, scope, timestamp, intended effect, evidence requirement and rollback path.
- Material-action classes: spend, pricing, billing, customer communication, deployment, access, data export, deletion.
- Default: recommendation only until approved.

Acceptance:
A reviewer can answer who approved what, why, what changed, and what proves completion.

## Layer 1 — Evidence and truth

Polish:
- Evidence is a first-class object, not a dashboard decoration.
- Every metric has source, period, definition, freshness and confidence.
- Live claims require a current verification event.

Activate:
- Evidence Ledger linked to every commercial decision.
- Evidence states: CAPTURED, VALIDATED, EXPIRED, REJECTED.
- Expiry rules for volatile evidence such as prices, availability, deployments and external status.

Acceptance:
No public or executive surface can turn an unverified state into a VERIFIED claim.

## Layer 2 — Data foundation

Polish:
- Use one canonical vocabulary for offer, customer, segment, source, event, cost and outcome.
- Preserve raw events separately from derived metrics.
- Never overwrite historical commercial facts.

Activate:
- Canonical entities: campaigns, channels, offers, segments, opportunities, customers, events, costs, experiments, evidence, decisions.
- Immutable event identifiers and timestamps.
- Derived profit snapshots are reproducible from source records.

Acceptance:
A profit figure can be reconstructed from its underlying revenue, cost and event records.

## Layer 3 — Profit economics

Polish:
- Replace vanity metrics with contribution economics.
- Make variable delivery/usage costs visible.
- Evaluate economics by OFFER × SEGMENT × SOURCE × PERIOD.

Activate:
- Revenue, direct delivery cost, variable infrastructure/usage cost, sales cost, marketing cost and transaction cost.
- Contribution profit, contribution margin, CAC, payback, retention, expansion and expected value.
- Owner-defined margin floors and escalation thresholds.

Acceptance:
Every material growth decision has a visible economic basis.

## Layer 4 — Demand and discovery

Polish:
- Treat search, answer-based discovery, referrals and direct demand as connected surfaces.
- Measure qualified commercial outcomes, not mentions alone.
- Make factual accuracy and proof coverage visible.

Activate:
- Query/intent library: category, problem, comparison, alternative and brand.
- Presence observations linked to source, date, claim, evidence and resulting commercial event where measurable.
- Competitive displacement is recorded as an opportunity, not assumed as a win.

Acceptance:
Discovery work can be traced from demand signal to qualified opportunity without inventing causality.

## Layer 5 — Evaluation and conversion

Polish:
- Move critical buying information earlier.
- Make the commercial path understandable without requiring unnecessary sales friction.
- Keep proof close to each claim.

Activate:
Evaluation Pack:
1. Problem and desired outcome
2. Scope and exclusions
3. Implementation path
4. Pricing and pricing logic
5. Expected economics / measurement method
6. Security and control evidence
7. Relevant proof
8. Risks and constraints
9. Next action

Conversion sequence:
PROBLEM → PROOF → FIT → OFFER → DECISION → PAYMENT/COMMITMENT

Acceptance:
A qualified buyer can understand what is being bought, what it changes, what it costs, and how success is verified.

## Layer 6 — Revenue recovery and lifecycle

Polish:
- Treat lost revenue as a recoverable operational queue.
- Separate genuine recovery from ordinary demand.
- Measure incremental revenue and incremental cost.

Activate:
Queues for abandoned enquiry, abandoned checkout, missed contact, dormant customer, renewal risk, expansion and referral.
Each event records trigger, attempted action, outcome, cost and evidence.

Acceptance:
Recovered revenue is not counted without an attributable lifecycle event and outcome record.

## Layer 7 — Experimentation and measurement

Polish:
- No single measurement method is treated as absolute.
- Separate optimization telemetry from causal proof.
- Escalate disagreements instead of hiding them.

Activate:
- Attribution for operational optimization.
- Incrementality/causal tests for material interventions.
- MMM/aggregate modeling for portfolio allocation when data volume and maturity justify it.
- Experiment registry with hypothesis, population, intervention, guardrails, duration, result and decision.

Acceptance:
A material allocation decision identifies whether it is based on attribution, causal evidence, aggregate modeling, or a clearly stated combination.

## Layer 8 — Capital allocation

Polish:
- Convert insight into a finite decision queue.
- Keep owner control over material capital movement.
- Show opportunity cost explicitly where measurable.

Activate:
Decision classes:
INCREASE / MAINTAIN / TEST / REPAIR / PAUSE / RETIRE

Each decision includes:
- proposed change
- expected economic effect
- evidence
- downside/risk
- required approval
- rollback condition
- verification condition

Acceptance:
No material spend change can occur without an explicit approval record.

## Layer 9 — Margin protection

Polish:
- Protect economics before growth creates hidden cost.
- Treat usage and fulfilment cost as first-class commercial inputs.

Activate:
- Margin Guard watches contribution margin and cost drift.
- Flags pricing, packaging, delivery or infrastructure changes.
- Distinguishes temporary variance from structural erosion.

Acceptance:
A deteriorating margin cannot remain invisible merely because revenue is growing.

## Layer 10 — Retention and expansion

Polish:
- Make the installed customer base an active economic surface.
- Measure renewal and expansion separately from acquisition.

Activate:
- Renewal health
- expansion opportunities
- cross-sell/upsell
- reactivation
- referral
- customer-level contribution

Acceptance:
Growth reporting shows acquisition and existing-customer economics separately.

## Layer 11 — Executive cockpit

Polish:
The cockpit should answer five questions immediately:
1. Where is money being made?
2. Where is money leaking?
3. What changed?
4. What requires my decision?
5. What has actually been proven?

Visual hierarchy:
- contribution first
- decision queue second
- evidence third
- operational detail on demand

Avoid decorative telemetry, false precision, excessive charts and internal implementation names on public surfaces.

Activate:
Core surfaces:
Profit Radar
Decision Queue
Revenue Recovery
Demand & Answer Presence
Conversion Observatory
Retention / Expansion
Experiment Lab
Margin Guard
Evidence Ledger

Acceptance:
The owner can move from signal → evidence → decision without hunting across systems.

## Layer 12 — Security and access

Polish:
- Public marketing, customer data, credentials and internal economics are separate trust zones.
- Least privilege is the default.
- Sensitive operations require explicit authorization.

Activate:
- role-based access
- protected secrets
- audit events
- approval-bound actions
- export controls
- deletion controls
- environment separation

Acceptance:
No public surface exposes credentials, customer-level economics, private evidence or internal control mechanisms.

## Layer 13 — Design system and experience

Visual direction:
Premium operational clarity with an unusual abstract visual language: deep mineral surfaces, precise luminous data traces, restrained motion, strong typography, and colorful evidence states.

Design rules:
- status is visually unambiguous
- evidence has provenance
- numbers have context
- actions are reversible where possible
- motion communicates state, never decoration alone
- dense data is progressively disclosed
- mobile remains decision-capable

Public brand rule:
Do not expose internal tool names or implementation jargon. Lead with outcomes, control, proof and commercial clarity.

Acceptance:
The interface feels distinctive but remains faster to operate than a conventional dashboard.

## Layer 14 — Deployment and verification

Polish:
- Separate build success from runtime success.
- Separate deployment success from business-function success.

Activate:
Verification chain:
SOURCE → BUILD → DEPLOY → HEALTH → FUNCTION → EVIDENCE → OWNER RECORD

Each release records:
commit/ref, environment, deployment result, runtime check, functional check, evidence and rollback reference.

Acceptance:
No deployment is called LIVE merely because a build succeeded.

## Layer 15 — Operating cadence

No permanent background automation is required.

Use explicit execution modes:
- MANUAL: owner starts the operation.
- EVENT-DRIVEN: a defined event triggers a bounded workflow.
- SCHEDULED: only when explicitly enabled by owner.

Every mode has:
scope, trigger, permissions, timeout, output, verification and stop condition.

## Definition of done

A layer is considered ACTIVE only when:
- its data contract exists,
- its control boundary exists,
- its user action exists,
- its verification method exists,
- its evidence is recorded,
- and its failure/rollback path is defined.

Otherwise its state is DESIGN, READY, BLOCKED or UNVERIFIED — never ACTIVE by implication.
