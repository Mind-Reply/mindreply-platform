# A11pro Layer Polish & Activation — 2026-09

## Objective
Turn the research and profit strategy into a controlled operating system that is useful immediately, measurable at every layer, visually coherent, secure by default, and never allowed to imply activity that has not been verified.

Operating rule:
VERIFY → PROVE → PROTECT → PROPOSE → OWNER APPROVE → EXECUTE → VERIFY → RECORD

No hidden background process, automatic spend, irreversible commercial change, or external commitment is implied by this design.

## Layer 0 — Authority and control
Polish: one owner identity, one approval vocabulary, one decision queue. Every material action has status PROPOSED, APPROVED, EXECUTING, VERIFIED, BLOCKED, FAILED or UNVERIFIED. Recommendation and execution are separate.
Activate: central approval record with actor, scope, timestamp, intended effect, evidence requirement and rollback path. Material classes include spend, pricing, billing, customer communication, deployment, access, data export and deletion.
Acceptance: a reviewer can answer who approved what, why, what changed and what proves completion.

## Layer 1 — Evidence and truth
Polish: evidence is a first-class object. Every metric has source, period, definition, freshness and confidence.
Activate: Evidence Ledger with CAPTURED, VALIDATED, EXPIRED and REJECTED states; expiry for volatile evidence.
Acceptance: no public or executive surface can turn an unverified state into a VERIFIED claim.

## Layer 2 — Data foundation
Polish: one canonical vocabulary; raw events remain separate from derived metrics; historical commercial facts are never overwritten.
Activate: canonical entities for campaigns, channels, offers, segments, opportunities, customers, events, costs, experiments, evidence and decisions; immutable event IDs and timestamps.
Acceptance: every profit figure is reconstructable from source records.

## Layer 3 — Profit economics
Polish: contribution economics replace vanity metrics and variable delivery/usage costs are visible.
Activate: revenue, direct delivery, variable infrastructure/usage, sales, marketing and transaction costs; contribution profit, margin, CAC, payback, retention, expansion and expected value; owner-defined margin floors.
Acceptance: every material growth decision has a visible economic basis.

## Layer 4 — Demand and discovery
Polish: connect search, answer-based discovery, referrals and direct demand; measure qualified outcomes rather than mentions.
Activate: query/intent library for category, problem, comparison, alternative and brand; observations linked to source, date, claim, evidence and commercial event where measurable.
Acceptance: discovery work can be traced to qualified opportunity without inventing causality.

## Layer 5 — Evaluation and conversion
Polish: move critical buying information earlier and put proof beside claims.
Activate Evaluation Pack: problem/outcome, scope/exclusions, implementation, pricing logic, expected economics, security/control evidence, proof, risks/constraints and next action.
Conversion: PROBLEM → PROOF → FIT → OFFER → DECISION → PAYMENT/COMMITMENT.
Acceptance: a qualified buyer can understand what is bought, changed, costs and how success is verified.

## Layer 6 — Revenue recovery and lifecycle
Polish: treat lost revenue as a recoverable operational queue; separate genuine recovery from ordinary demand.
Activate queues for abandoned enquiry, abandoned checkout, missed contact, dormant customer, renewal risk, expansion and referral. Record trigger, action, outcome and cost.
Acceptance: recovered revenue requires an attributable lifecycle event and outcome.

## Layer 7 — Experimentation and measurement
Polish: no single measurement method is absolute; optimization telemetry and causal proof are distinct.
Activate attribution for optimization, incrementality/causal tests for material interventions, and MMM/aggregate modeling for portfolio allocation when mature enough. Experiment registry records hypothesis, population, intervention, guardrails, duration, result and decision.
Acceptance: each material allocation decision states its evidence basis.

## Layer 8 — Capital allocation
Polish: convert insight into a finite decision queue and keep owner control over material capital movement.
Activate decisions INCREASE / MAINTAIN / TEST / REPAIR / PAUSE / RETIRE. Each contains proposed change, expected effect, evidence, downside, approval, rollback and verification conditions.
Acceptance: no material spend change without explicit approval.

## Layer 9 — Margin protection
Polish: protect economics before growth hides cost.
Activate Margin Guard for contribution margin and cost drift; distinguish temporary variance from structural erosion.
Acceptance: revenue growth cannot mask material margin deterioration.

## Layer 10 — Retention and expansion
Polish: treat the installed customer base as an economic surface and separate renewal/expansion from acquisition.
Activate renewal health, expansion, cross-sell/upsell, reactivation, referral and customer contribution.
Acceptance: acquisition and existing-customer economics are reported separately.

## Layer 11 — Executive cockpit
The cockpit answers: where money is made, where money leaks, what changed, what requires a decision and what has been proven.
Visual hierarchy: contribution first, decision queue second, evidence third, detail on demand.
Surfaces: Profit Radar, Decision Queue, Revenue Recovery, Demand & Answer Presence, Conversion Observatory, Retention/Expansion, Experiment Lab, Margin Guard, Evidence Ledger.
Acceptance: owner can move from signal to evidence to decision without hunting across systems.

## Layer 12 — Security and access
Polish: public marketing, customer data, credentials and internal economics are separate trust zones.
Activate least privilege, protected secrets, audit events, approval-bound actions, export controls, deletion controls and environment separation.
Acceptance: no public surface exposes credentials, customer economics, private evidence or internal control mechanisms.

## Layer 13 — Design system and experience
Visual direction: premium operational clarity with an unusual abstract language—deep mineral surfaces, precise luminous data traces, restrained motion, strong typography and colorful evidence states.
Rules: status is unambiguous; evidence has provenance; numbers have context; actions are reversible where possible; motion communicates state; dense data is progressively disclosed; mobile remains decision-capable.
Public brand rule: do not expose internal tool names or implementation jargon. Lead with outcomes, control, proof and commercial clarity.
Acceptance: distinctive without becoming slower than a conventional dashboard.

## Layer 14 — Deployment and verification
Polish: build success, runtime success and business-function success are separate.
Activate verification chain: SOURCE → BUILD → DEPLOY → HEALTH → FUNCTION → EVIDENCE → OWNER RECORD.
Each release records commit/ref, environment, deployment result, runtime check, functional check, evidence and rollback reference.
Acceptance: no deployment is called LIVE merely because a build succeeded.

## Layer 15 — Operating cadence
MANUAL: owner starts the operation. EVENT-DRIVEN: defined event triggers a bounded workflow. SCHEDULED: only when explicitly enabled by owner.
Every mode has scope, trigger, permissions, timeout, output, verification and stop condition.

## Definition of done
A layer is ACTIVE only when its data contract, control boundary, user action, verification method, evidence record and failure/rollback path exist. Otherwise its state is DESIGN, READY, BLOCKED or UNVERIFIED—never ACTIVE by implication.
